Forecasting Pedagogy Simulations

Three self-contained interactive classroom simulations. All logic is client-side and seeded for reproducible classroom runs.

  • Fermi Forge — breaking big questions down: decomposition, error cancellation, sensitivity analysis.
  • Belief Lab — actively open-minded thinking: evidence search, updating symmetry, structural fixes.
  • Fox & Hedgehog Derby — cognitive style and forecasting accuracy: tournament, fame meter, team building.
